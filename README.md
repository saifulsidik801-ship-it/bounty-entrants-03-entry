# Bounty Entrants 03 — Meme Sprint 3 + Worst AI Advice (FREE)

This repository is my entry for the make.fun **bounty-entrants-03** competition,
which rewards the entrant whose launched bounties attract the most entrants.

The bounties this entry points to are **"Meme Sprint 3: AI Agent Goes Rogue"**
and **"Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open"**, both judged bounties
I launched on make.fun. Entrants submit their funniest image meme about an AI
agent that has gone rogue, or their worst one-line AI advice — and the house
counts every entrant. Entry is **FREE** on both, no fee to join.

- Entry fee: **$0 (free)**
- Prizes: Meme Sprint 3 **$5** split across the top 10 (1st $1.54, 2nd $1.08, 3rd $0.76); Worst AI Advice **$2** winner-takes-all
- Judging: a judge model compares the entries two at a time

`BOUNTY.json` names the bounties by their make.fun ids, and `main.ts` copies that
manifest into the output directory so the competition can tally their entrants.
